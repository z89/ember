# changelog

## 0.2.0

- mode changes fade. the screen warms or cools over 800ms instead of snapping, and DankMaterialShell (DMS) takes over at the value the fade lands on
- fade length is a plugin setting, 0 for instant
- the pill, panel and control center tile show where a fade is heading while it runs

## 0.1.0

first release.

- bar pill with icon, live kelvin readout and warm tint
- panel with a three way switch for always on, scheduled and off
- night and day sliders on one shared 2000K to 6500K range
- live preview while dragging, saved to DMS state 1200ms after you let go
- control center tile
- right click on the pill flips between always on and scheduled
